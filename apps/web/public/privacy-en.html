Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 1, 2026

Quberi ("we", "us", or "the Service") takes your privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your information when you use the Quberi enterprise knowledge and conversation platform (ai.quberi.com).

1. Information We Collect

  • Account information: the email address, phone number, and display name you provide at sign-up, plus basic identity information (name, email, avatar) obtained through third-party sign-in (e.g. Google, Feishu).
  • Content data: documents you upload to your knowledge base, conversation messages, and the vector embeddings generated from them to support retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) question answering.
  • Usage data: login records, operation logs, and API call metadata, used for security auditing and service reliability.
  • Audit log: organization administrators can see members' activity records in the audit log, including metadata about activity in a member's personal space (such as file names and times). It does not include the text of documents or conversations.
  • Device and technical information: IP address, browser type, and access times, used for security protection and troubleshooting.

2. How We Use Information

  • To provide, maintain, and improve core features (document retrieval, conversational Q&A, team collaboration).
  • To perform identity verification, access control, and account security protection.
  • To send you service notifications (e.g. verification codes, important change notices).
  • To troubleshoot issues and analyze usage patterns to improve system performance and stability.

3. Third-Party Processing

To power conversational Q&A, your questions and the retrieved document excerpts may be sent to the large language model provider configured by you or your organization (for example DeepSeek, or any other OpenAI-compatible service you bind under "AI Providers") in order to generate a response. We do not use your document content to train third-party public models. When you sign in with Google or Feishu, we only receive the minimum identity information required for login and do not access any other data on those platforms.

4. Data Storage and Security

  • Document originals and index data are stored within your organization's isolated space; different organizations are strictly isolated from one another.
  • Secrets and sensitive credentials (such as third-party API keys) are stored encrypted in our database.
  • We take reasonable technical and organizational measures (access control, encryption in transit) to protect your data, but cannot guarantee absolute security.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

You may delete uploaded documents at any time from "My Knowledge"; the corresponding index data is removed in step. After account deletion, we will delete or anonymize your personal information within a reasonable period, except where retention is required by law.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal information, or to request that we restrict its processing. To exercise these rights, please contact us using the details below.

7. Policy Updates

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be communicated via in-app notice or email. Continued use of the Service after an update constitutes your acceptance of the revised policy.
